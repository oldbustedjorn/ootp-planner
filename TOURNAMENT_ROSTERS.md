## Notes

- The standard PT regular-season and main PT lineup plans are intentionally excluded.
- This lists 23 active tournament rosters: 2 Quick, 14 Daily, and 7 Weekly.
- The deleted one-time PTCS Cap roster is no longer listed.
- Older labels such as `T-021`, and date suffixes such as `1002` or `1006`, are
  roster labels rather than OOTP tournament IDs.
- Tournament rules can change. Before entering, match the tournament title and
  restrictions above; do not treat an instance-ID change by itself as a rules change.
